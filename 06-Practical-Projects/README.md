# 06 — Practical Projects

Practice complete business scenarios using fictional data. These projects connect ERP fundamentals, Odoo 18 Community, AI, and no-code thinking.

**No programming or coding exercises.** Use paper, diagrams.net, Google Sheets, and free AI tools where appropriate.

## Project 1 — Understand a trading business

**Scenario:** A fictional wholesaler sells office supplies from one warehouse.

Tasks:
- Draw the journey from customer inquiry to payment.
- Identify the Sales, Purchase, Inventory, and Accounting activities.
- List five questions to understand the current process.
- Explain the business problem an ERP system could solve.

**Deliverable:** one process map and one-page explanation.

## Project 2 — Map the process to Odoo 18

**Scenario:** The wholesaler wants to track leads, quotations, confirmed orders, and deliveries.

Tasks:
- Identify the relevant Odoo apps.
- Write the steps a sales user follows.
- Explain what information moves between Sales and Inventory.
- Check the official Odoo 18 documentation for edition and feature details.

**Deliverable:** a simple app-to-process table. Do not change a real Odoo database.

## Project 3 — Prepare an AI-assisted discovery pack

**Scenario:** A fictional retailer wants to replace spreadsheets with a business system.

Tasks:
- Ask an AI assistant to explain unfamiliar ERP terms.
- Draft eight short discovery questions.
- Ask AI to organize fictional notes into a requirements checklist.
- Review every answer and correct unsupported claims.

**Deliverable:** a discovery question list and requirements summary.

## Project 4 — Design a no-code follow-up workflow

**Scenario:** A new inquiry arrives through a sample online form.

Tasks:
- Draw the trigger and actions.
- Show where a human reviews the inquiry.
- Identify which tools might connect on free plans.
- Note privacy, permissions, and usage limits.

**Deliverable:** one visual workflow. Building the live connection is optional and only for sample data.

## Project 5 — Present a mini ERP solution

**Scenario:** A fictional distributor has 10 users, one warehouse, and needs Sales, Purchase, and Inventory visibility.

Tasks:
- Summarize the business situation.
- Map needs to possible ERP areas.
- List assumptions and questions still unanswered.
- Prepare a short demo agenda.
- Explain what must be validated before a quotation is issued.

**Deliverable:** a two-page solution outline.

## Project review checklist

- [ ] I used fictional data only.
- [ ] I explained technical terms in simple English and Arabic.
- [ ] I connected each suggested app to a real business need.
- [ ] I checked Odoo Community edition limitations.
- [ ] I did not use paid tools or features.
- [ ] I did not write code.
- [ ] I can explain the result to a non-technical business owner.
