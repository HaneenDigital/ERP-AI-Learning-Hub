# 03 — AI Tools

Use AI as a learning and work assistant. You do not need to build or program AI models.

## What is AI?

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** means computer systems that can perform tasks such as understanding language, summarizing information, or generating drafts.

**Generative AI (الذكاء الاصطناعي التوليدي)** can create text, images, or other content in response to instructions.

## Beginner skills

- Ask clear questions and provide enough non-confidential context.
- Ask for simple English and an Arabic explanation of technical terms.
- Request examples related to ERP Sales and Odoo 18 Community.
- Ask AI to organize notes into tables and checklists.
- Verify important claims using official documentation.

## A simple prompt pattern

Use this structure:

1. **Role:** “Act as an ERP functional consultant.”
2. **Task:** “Explain the difference between a lead and an opportunity.”
3. **Context:** “I am a beginner learning Odoo 18 Community.”
4. **Format:** “Use simple English, Egyptian Arabic explanations, and one fictional business example.”

### Example prompt

“Explain the quotation-to-sales-order process in Odoo 18 Community for a fictional trading company. I am a beginner. Use simple English, explain technical terms in Arabic, and do not include programming.”

## Practical ERP Sales uses

- Turn fictional discovery notes into a requirements checklist.
- Prepare short, non-repetitive discovery questions.
- Explain an ERP term before a customer call.
- Summarize public product documentation.
- Practice answering common customer questions.

## Safety and accuracy

- Never paste private customer, employee, company, financial, or login information into an AI tool.
- Use fictional names and invented sample figures.
- AI can make mistakes. Verify Odoo edition features, pricing, and technical claims with reliable sources.
- Do not let AI send messages or make business commitments without your review.

## Practice — no coding

Ask a free AI assistant to explain CRM, Inventory, and Purchase in simple English and Arabic. Then ask it to create five discovery questions for a fictional wholesale business. Review the questions yourself.
