# 02 — Odoo 18 Community

Learn Odoo as a **functional user**, not as a programmer.

## What is Odoo?

Odoo is business management software with connected apps. **Community** is its open-source edition. Some features differ between Community and Enterprise, so always verify edition availability.

## Apps to recognize

| App / Area | What it helps with | المعنى بالعربي |
|---|---|---|
| CRM | Track leads and sales opportunities | متابعة العملاء المحتملين والفرص |
| Sales | Quotations and sales orders | عروض الأسعار وأوامر البيع |
| Purchase | Supplier quotations and purchase orders | طلبات الشراء والموردين |
| Inventory | Receipts, deliveries, and stock | الاستلام والتسليم والمخزون |
| Invoicing / Accounting | Customer invoices and financial records; exact availability depends on edition and setup | الفواتير والسجلات المالية |
| Point of Sale (POS) | In-store sales | البيع من نقطة البيع |

## A basic sales workflow

For a fictional company:

1. Create or open a customer record.
2. Record an opportunity in CRM (فرصة بيع).
3. Prepare a quotation in Sales (عرض سعر).
4. Confirm it as a sales order (أمر بيع) when accepted.
5. Check delivery and stock operations in Inventory.
6. Review the invoicing process supported by the installed edition and configuration.

The exact menus and available features depend on the Odoo 18 Community installation and installed modules.

## Important terms

- **Database (قاعدة البيانات):** the place where the system stores business records.
- **Configuration (الإعدادات):** choices that adapt the system to the business.
- **User Access (صلاحيات المستخدم):** what each person is allowed to see or do.
- **Product:** an item or service sold or purchased.
- **Quotation:** a proposed price and scope sent to a customer.
- **Sales Order:** the confirmed customer order.

## Practice — no coding

- Explore an authorized Odoo 18 Community demo or practice database.
- Find the CRM, Sales, Purchase, and Inventory areas if available.
- Follow a fictional quotation from creation to confirmation.
- Write down what information Sales needs from Inventory.
- Do not install modules or change settings in a real company database without permission.

## Beginner reminder

You do not need to write Python, XML, or SQL to learn functional Odoo workflows. Focus on what the business needs, what the user does, and what result the system should produce.
