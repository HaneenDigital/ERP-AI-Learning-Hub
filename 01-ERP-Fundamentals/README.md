# 01 — ERP Fundamentals

**Start here.** Learn how a business works before learning software screens.

## What you will learn

- What ERP means and why businesses use it.
- How Sales, CRM, Purchasing, Inventory, Accounting, and Operations connect.
- The difference between a business process and a software feature.
- Common ERP words in simple English and Arabic.

## Key terms

| Term | Simple English | الشرح بالعربي |
|---|---|---|
| ERP | One system connecting business activities and information | نظام بيربط إدارات الشركة وبياناتها |
| Department | A team responsible for a business function | إدارة زي المبيعات أو الحسابات |
| Process | A series of steps to complete work | خطوات تنفيذ مهمة |
| Master Data | Main records used repeatedly, such as products and customers | بيانات أساسية بنستخدمها باستمرار |
| Transaction | A recorded business activity | حركة أو عملية اتسجلت في النظام |
| Stock | Products a business owns or stores | البضاعة الموجودة في المخزن |
| Receivable | Money a customer owes the business | فلوس مستحقة للشركة عند العميل |
| Payable | Money the business owes a supplier | فلوس الشركة مطالبة بدفعها للمورد |

## Example: a simple trading company

Imagine a fictional company called **Bright Supplies**.

1. A customer asks for a price. Sales records the request.
2. Sales prepares a quotation (عرض سعر).
3. The customer confirms the order.
4. Inventory checks whether the products are available.
5. The warehouse prepares and delivers the products.
6. Accounting issues an invoice and records the payment.

This connected process is often called **Order to Cash** (من طلب العميل لحد تحصيل الفلوس).
