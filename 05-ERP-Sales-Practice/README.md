# 05 — ERP Sales Practice

Turn ERP knowledge into clear customer conversations and useful requirements.

## The sales discovery journey

1. **Prepare:** understand the prospect's public business activity.
2. **Discover:** ask how the business works today.
3. **Qualify:** understand urgency, scope, users, branches, and decision process.
4. **Map requirements:** connect business needs to ERP areas.
5. **Validate:** confirm what is standard, what needs configuration, and what needs further technical review.
6. **Next step:** agree on a demo, proposal, or follow-up.

## Beginner-friendly terms

| Term | Simple English | الشرح بالعربي |
|---|---|---|
| Lead | A person or business that may become a customer | عميل محتمل |
| Opportunity | A qualified possible sale being actively followed | فرصة بيع |
| Discovery | Questions to understand the business and its needs | مكالمة فهم وتحليل الاحتياج |
| Qualification | Checking whether the opportunity is a good fit | تقييم جدية ومناسبة الفرصة |
| Scope | What is included in the project | نطاق المشروع |
| Stakeholder | A person affected by or involved in the decision | شخص له علاقة بالمشروع أو القرار |
| Demo | A demonstration of the software | عرض عملي للنظام |
| Proposal | A document describing the proposed solution and price | عرض فني وتجاري |

## Discovery questions for a fictional trading business

- How do you record sales today?
- How many users need to work in the system?
- How many branches or warehouses do you have?
- How do you check product availability before confirming an order?
- How do you manage purchasing and supplier orders?
- Which reports do managers need regularly?
- What is the main problem you want the new system to solve?
- When would you like to start?

Ask follow-up questions only when an answer needs clarification. Do not overwhelm the customer with a long checklist in one conversation.

## Map needs to ERP areas

| Customer need | Possible ERP area |
|---|---|
| Follow up with prospects | CRM |
| Prepare quotations and sales orders | Sales |
| Reorder products from suppliers | Purchase |
| Track stock and deliveries | Inventory |
| Record invoices and payments | Invoicing / Accounting |
| Sell in a physical shop | POS |

This is an initial mapping, not a promise that every feature is available in every edition or setup.

## Practice — no coding

Use a fictional wholesale company with 12 users, one warehouse, and a spreadsheet-based process.

- Write a short call opening.
- Ask five discovery questions.
- Summarize the business problem in three sentences.
- Map the needs to possible Odoo apps.
- List what must be checked before promising a feature or price.

## Professional boundaries

Never place real customer names, contact details, quotations, internal prices, or company documents in this public learning repository. Do not promise functionality, delivery dates, or prices before the responsible team validates them.
