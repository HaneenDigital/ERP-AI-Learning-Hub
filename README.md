# ERP-AI-Learning-Hub

A beginner-friendly, practical learning space for ERP, Odoo 18 Community, AI tools, and no-code automation.

**Learning style:** no programming, no coding exercises, free tools and resources, simple English with Arabic explanations, and realistic examples from ERP Sales.

## 1. My learning goals

By the end of this learning journey, I want to be able to:

- Explain what an ERP system does and how departments work together.
- Understand common business processes: sales, purchasing, inventory, accounting, CRM, and customer support.
- Navigate Odoo 18 Community as a functional user and understand its main apps and workflows.
- Ask better discovery questions and translate customer needs into ERP requirements.
- Use free AI tools responsibly to research, summarize, prepare questions, and organize work.
- Build simple visual automations with no-code tools, without writing scripts.
- Explain solutions clearly to business owners and decision-makers.

## 2. How to use this hub

1. Start with **01-ERP-Fundamentals**. Do not rush into software screens before understanding the business process.
2. Follow the sections in order, then revisit them whenever a customer scenario needs them.
3. Study one small topic at a time. Write a short explanation in your own words.
4. Use fictional company names and sample data only. Never add private company, employee, prospect, or customer information.
5. Practice by drawing workflows, making checklists, and explaining scenarios. No coding is required.
6. Prefer official documentation and free plans. A free plan may have usage limits; never assume a paid feature is free.

## 3. Beginner-friendly roadmap

| Stage | Focus | What I should be able to do |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | ERP fundamentals | Explain ERP and map a simple order-to-cash process |
| Weeks 3–4 | Odoo 18 Community | Recognize core apps and follow a basic sales-to-delivery flow |
| Weeks 5–6 | ERP Sales practice | Run a short discovery conversation and document requirements |
| Weeks 7–8 | AI tools | Use AI to prepare, summarize, and check work safely |
| Weeks 9–10 | No-code automation | Design a simple trigger-and-action workflow visually |
| Weeks 11–12 | Practical projects | Present a small end-to-end business scenario and its proposed solution |

Move at a comfortable pace. Understanding the business reason behind a step is more important than memorizing menu names.

## 5. Weekly learning plan

Aim for **4–5 hours per week**, adjusting the time to fit your schedule.

| Day | Suggested activity | Time |
|---|---|---:|
| Day 1 | Learn one new concept and note its Arabic meaning | 40 min |
| Day 2 | Read the related official guide or watch a free lesson | 40 min |
| Day 3 | Map a business process on paper or in diagrams.net | 40 min |
| Day 4 | Apply the idea to a fictional ERP Sales / Odoo example | 40 min |
| Day 5 | Explain what you learned in simple English and Arabic; review gaps | 40 min |

At the end of each week, answer:
- What does this concept mean in simple words?
- Which business problem does it solve?
- Where might I see it in Odoo 18 Community?
- What would I ask a customer to understand their needs?

## 6. Free learning tools and resources

These resources have free access or free tiers. Some features, usage, or hosting options may require payment; check current terms before using them.

- **Odoo documentation:** https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/ — official Odoo 18 reference.
- **Odoo Community source and information:** https://www.odoo.com/page/community — learn about the Community ecosystem.
- **Odoo eLearning:** https://www.odoo.com/slides — browse available lessons; access conditions can vary.
- **diagrams.net:** https://www.diagrams.net/ — draw process maps visually.
- **Google Sheets:** https://www.google.com/sheets/about/ — organize fictional sample requirements and checklists.
- **ChatGPT:** https://chatgpt.com/ — use the free plan for explanations and practice, subject to limits.
- **Make:** https://www.make.com/ — visual no-code automation with a free plan and usage limits.
- **n8n:** https://n8n.io/ — visual workflow automation; self-hosting is an option but may require technical setup.
- **HubSpot Academy:** https://academy.hubspot.com/ — free sales and CRM learning resources.

## 7. Important learning rules

- No programming, code snippets, scripts, or coding homework.
- Do not pay for a course or subscription just to complete this roadmap.
- Do not paste confidential data into AI tools or public learning files.
- Treat AI output as a draft: verify facts, prices, product capabilities, and technical details.
- Odoo Community and Odoo Enterprise are different editions. Always check whether a feature is available in the Community edition before promising it.
- A functional consultant focuses on business processes, requirements, configuration, and user guidance; custom development is a separate skill.
